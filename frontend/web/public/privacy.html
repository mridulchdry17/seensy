Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Seensy is a browser extension for Gmail that tells you when an email you sent is opened and when links in it are clicked. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and how you can delete it. Contact: mridul17chaudhary@gmail.com.

in short

  • We know your name and email from Google sign-in, nothing from your inbox.
  • For emails you track, we store the subject, recipients, links, and when they were opened or clicked.
  • We never store the body of your emails, and we never sell data.
  • Email us from your account's address and we delete everything within 30 days.

Information we collect

WhatDetailsWhy
Your account Google email address, name and Google account ID, from Google sign-in To create your account and show it in the extension
Tracked emails Subject, recipient addresses, Gmail thread ID, the time sent, and each link's address and visible text To show which email was opened and which link was clicked
Opens and clicks Time, the browser or mail app's user agent, and the country derived from the network address. The network address itself is not stored. To report opens and clicks, and to filter out mail scanners and your own views
Your own views The time you open one of your own tracked emails in Gmail So your own views are never counted as the recipient's
Early-access list The email address you enter on our website, the time, and which form you used To tell you when Seensy is available. We send nothing else to this address.
Session data A session token per signed-in browser (we store only a hash of it) and a random owner key stored in a cookie on our tracking domain To keep you signed in and recognise clicks on your own links

What we never collect

  • The body, attachments or images of your emails.
  • Your inbox, other emails, contacts or calendar. Seensy does not use the Gmail API.
  • Anything from emails you send with tracking switched off.

How tracking works

When you send an email with tracking on, the extension adds an invisible 1×1 image and routes each link through our server, which redirects to the original address. When the recipient's mail app loads the image or the recipient clicks a link, our server records the event for you.

Google sign-in

Seensy requests only the openid, email and profile scopes. We use this information only to identify your account. Seensy's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who processes your data

  • Cloudflare hosts our server and database.
  • Google provides sign-in.
  • InboxSDK (by Streak), the library the extension uses inside Gmail, sends error reports and usage events to its own servers.
  • Vercel hosts this website.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

People you email

Seensy records when the people you email open your message or click its links, together with their email address as you entered it. Recipients who prefer not to be tracked can block remote images in their mail app. A recipient can ask us to delete data about them by writing to the contact address above.

Data stored in your browser

The extension keeps your session token, your settings and a short queue of pending updates in the browser's extension storage. Signing out removes the session from that browser.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. Email the contact address from your Google account's address to have your account and all its tracking data deleted; we do this within 30 days. Uninstalling the extension stops all tracking but does not delete data already stored.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Session tokens are stored only as hashes. Access to the database is limited to the Seensy operator.

Children

Seensy is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes.
