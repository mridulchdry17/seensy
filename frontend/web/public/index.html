FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Seensy read my emails?

No. Seensy only asks Google for your name and email address. It adds tracking to emails you choose when you press Send, and stores their subject, recipients and links, never the message body.

How does it know when someone opens my email?

Seensy adds an invisible image to the email. When the recipient's mail app loads it, we record an open. Links go through Seensy first, so a click is recorded and then opens the real page straight away.

How accurate are opens?

Clicks are very reliable. Opens are a strong signal but not perfect: some apps block images (missed opens), Apple Mail can load them automatically (extra opens), and Gmail caches images, so a second open isn't always counted. Seensy labels hits in the first minute as "early" because they're often security scanners.

Will the recipient know?

The email looks exactly as you wrote it. Hovering over a link shows that it goes through Seensy before the real page.

Does it work on my phone?

You send from Gmail on a computer in Chrome, Brave or Edge. Recipients can open your email anywhere, including their phone, and it's tracked.

Can I switch it off for one email?

Yes. Click the ✓✓ switch next to Send before sending. You can also make "off" the default in the extension.

How do I delete my data?

Email us from your account's address and we'll delete your account and all tracking data within 30 days. Details are in the privacy policy.
