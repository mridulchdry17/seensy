Works inside the Gmail you already use

  1. Sign in with Google

    Seensy only sees your email address and name. It never asks to read or send your mail.

  2. Send as usual

    A ✓✓ switch next to Send tracks that email. Links you add are tracked automatically.

  3. See who's interested

    Ticks in your Sent folder and a live feed show every open and every click on your résumé.

Built to know as little about you as possible

What Seensy keeps

  • Your Google email address and name
  • For tracked emails: subject, recipient addresses and the links in them
  • When a tracked email is opened or a link is clicked

What Seensy never touches

  • The body of your emails
  • Your inbox, attachments or contacts
  • Any email you don't choose to track
